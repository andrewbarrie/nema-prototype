Helpful tip
Some insurers exclude flood entirely in high-risk areas, or define "flood" differently to storm water. Always read the Product Disclosure Statement (PDS), not just the quote summary.
01 / Understand

Know your hazards

Every property sits within a flood zone, bushfire zone, cyclone or storm surge area. Most buyers don't check until it's too late.

02 / Prepare

Ask the right questions

Before you make an offer, there are specific checks, reports, and conversations with your insurer that can save you tens of thousands.

03 / Protect

Cover the gaps

Insurance definitions matter. A policy that covers "storm" may not cover "flood." Understanding the difference is the difference.
